## V1–V25 version notes

- V1 — Classic 3-Column Blog: traditional blog IA. Focuses on a complete, familiar structure to start from.
- V2 — Editorial Magazine Blog: magazine-like typography. Focuses on reading rhythm and hierarchy.
- V3 — Dark Hacker Blog: terminal-like dark theme. Focuses on strong identity and mood.
- V4 — Japanese Minimal Blog: calm paper tone and fine lines. Focuses on restraint and whitespace.
- V5 — Vintage Newspaper Blog: aged paper texture and newspaper masthead structure. Focuses on editorial storytelling.
- V6 — Cyberpunk Neon Blog: high-contrast gradients and neon accents. Focuses on impact and tech atmosphere.
- V7 — Hand-Drawn Nature Blog: dashed dividers and warm, friendly palette. Focuses on a lifestyle approachable tone.
- V8 — Corporate Newsletter Blog: newsletter-style blocks and scanning-friendly layout. Focuses on business readability.
- V9 — Classic Layout, Dark Palette: classic structure with a darker theme. Explores a more serious steady reading feel.
- V10 — Editorial Grid, Warm Palette: grid-driven editorial layout with warmer tones. Explores sectioned content and warmth.
- V11 — Hover Card Blog: card interactions via hover states. Explores interaction feel without JavaScript.
- V12 — Identity-First Blog: puts author brand up front. Explores the weight of author card and CTAs in the layout.
- V13 — Card Waterfall Blog: higher-density cards and waterfall-like layout. Tests content density vs readability.
- V14 — Fixed Left Sidebar Blog: fixed author sidebar direction. Validates stable identity column and scrollable content.
- V15 — Soft Gradient Blog: soft gradients with a card system. Evaluates how light decoration affects perceived quality.
- V16 — Academic Research Blog: academic or paper style. Explores summaries, citations, and structured reading tools.
- V17 — Core Direction: locks the main direction.
- V18 — Typography Refinement: strengthens hierarchy and introduces a featured hero pattern on top of V17.
- V19 — Author Brand Refinement: polishes the author area.
- V20 — Visual Consistency: standardizes the design system as the final foundation.
- V21 — Responsive Breakpoints: defines responsive breakpoints and collapse strategy: ≤1024 / ≤768 / mobile.
- V22 — Write Module: isolates the writing or publishing entry; finalized as `index.html#write`.
- V23 — Post Module: isolates the single-post reading experience; finalized as `index.html#post`.
- V24 — About Module: isolates the author profile; finalized as `index.html#about`.
- V25 — Final: the converged final version with single-page anchors, a unified visual system, dependency-free deployment, and complete responsiveness.
